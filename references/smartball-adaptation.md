# SMARTBALL AI adaptation

Use this reference when applying Hallmark to SMARTBALL AI.

## Product job

Help someone understand what has changed in artificial intelligence, why it matters, and what to explore or learn next.

## Product-specific visual language

Show real relationships between signals, sources, companies, models, technologies, consequences, and learning resources. Do not use circuit decoration or node maps that have no navigable content behind them.

## Screen jobs

- **Home:** what changed and why it matters.
- **Discover:** which current signals deserve attention.
- **Understand:** how the pieces of the AI system relate.
- **Learn:** which capability or concept the user can acquire.
- **Apply:** what the user can do with the knowledge.

## Signature pattern

Use a functional context chain when it clarifies a story:

`signal → source → entity → technology → consequence → related resource`

Each step needs a real destination or explanation. If a relationship is unknown, do not draw it.

## Constraints

Preserve the favicon, SMARTBALL AI name, bilingual ES/EN support, deep content, existing routes, and factual source links. Do not let My AI Path dominate the home unless the user's brief explicitly changes that priority.

## Direction to avoid

Do not make the product a newspaper clone, a generic dark dashboard, a collection of identical cards, or a neon AI cliché. Use editorial judgement, but make the system and relationships visible through real content.
